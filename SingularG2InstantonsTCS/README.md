## Status and provenance

Both PDFs are AI-assisted working papers under author review. Human certification and peer review are pending. The PDFs were compiled from Overleaf revision `bee84f3b28295b0bf8b69e0b5a7dfe7fcd77db81` and carry the Lab's working-paper watermark.

Paper I develops singular instanton gluing for inelastic data and gives a compact example with one singular circle. Paper II develops equivariant gluing with nonzero relative deformation space and applies it to a compact example from the Hartshorne–Serre construction.

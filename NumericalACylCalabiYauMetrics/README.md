## Status and provenance

This is an AI-assisted working paper under author review. The authors proposed the research project to Claude Fable and steered it throughout; this version is the initial AI-generated draft of the results, which the authors are refining. Human certification and peer review are pending. The PDF carries the Lab's working-paper watermark.

The paper computes numerical approximations of asymptotically cylindrical (ACyl) Calabi–Yau metrics, the building blocks of the twisted connected sum construction of compact G₂-manifolds. For diagonal pencils it proves that the end is an exact product, that the cylinder constant is an explicit period, and that the integral compatibility condition reduces to a single coefficient given by a convergent flux integral. This allows a global neural-network Kähler potential without truncation, tested on model problems with exact solutions, on an ALH hyperkähler surface, and on the quartic-pencil building block of ℙ³, whose cross-section is the Fermat quartic K3 surface. The numerical results are evidence; no rigorous error bound is claimed.

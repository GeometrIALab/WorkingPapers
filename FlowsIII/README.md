## Overview

The manuscript studies Ricci–harmonic flows of tensorial structures with fixed natural quadratic torsion terms. It establishes stability near full-holonomy torsion-free G₂ and Spin(7) structures on closed manifolds, retained local regularity, pointed compactness including finite-regularity initial traces, and continuation criteria based on a continuous metric endpoint and torsion integrability or on the integrability of the full velocity.

For the modified Ricci–harmonic family, it develops coefficient-dependent soliton and dissipation identities, long-time convergence under the stated coercivity and noncollapse hypotheses, a critical-parameter steady G₂ classification, explicit Heisenberg and cylindrical solitons, and a finite homogeneous catalogue. It also constructs collapsing Type I singularities, determines SU(3), Type IIA and product comparisons, and proves gradient-flow obstructions within the specified functional, pairing and gauge classes.

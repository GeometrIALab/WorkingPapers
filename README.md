# GeometrIA Lab — Working Papers

This repository hosts working papers developed at [**GeometrIA Lab**](https://www.cbg.ime.unicamp.br/GeometrIALab/en/). It provides a place to share preliminary manuscripts, preserve dated versions, and document ongoing research before submission to arXiv or other publication venues.

## Manuscript status

These papers are works in progress. They may contain incomplete arguments, provisional results, or sections that change substantially between versions. Inclusion in this repository does not imply peer review.

Please consult the version and date of each manuscript when reading, discussing, or citing it.

## Standard procedure: authorship and human certification

GeometrIA Lab follows the procedure below for papers emerging from its AI pipeline.

1. **Time-stamped working paper.** As soon as a manuscript emerges from the AI pipeline, make it available through the [Lab website](https://www.cbg.ime.unicamp.br/GeometrIALab/) and this working-paper repository. Identify the version, date, AI provenance, contributors and current review status. Preserve a permanent, time-stamped record of each released version. This initial release documents the work and its development; it does not certify mathematical correctness or imply human validation or peer review.

2. **Expert inspection and coauthorship.** Invite experts from the mathematical community to inspect the argument, identify and resolve gaps, correct or develop the manuscript, and join as coauthors. The participating human authors review and approve the resulting manuscript and assume responsibility for its mathematical content. This is the human certification stage before submission to arXiv. An invitation alone does not establish coauthorship, and an AI-generated working paper must not be described as human-certified before this stage has been completed.

3. **arXiv and journal submission.** Once the human authors have approved the manuscript, submit it to arXiv and subsequently to a peer-reviewed journal. Update the working-paper record with links to these later versions and their status. Expert inspection and author approval are distinct from subsequent independent journal peer review; an arXiv posting does not itself constitute peer review.

The working-paper timestamp records provenance and chronology. Human certification records the authors' review and responsibility. Journal peer review is a separate later assessment. Descriptions of a paper must accurately reflect the stage it has actually reached.

## Citing a working paper

Please cite the specific version you consulted and use a permanent link to its commit or release whenever possible.

Suggested format:

> Author(s). “Paper title.” GeometrIA Lab Working Paper, version X, YYYY-MM-DD. URL.

If a later arXiv or published version is available, consider citing it unless your discussion specifically concerns an earlier draft.
